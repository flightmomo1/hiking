CH6.5.5 300s movement QA gate consumption v1 is a downstream consumption gate layer only. It does not compute or authorize radar scores, ability scores, ability ranks, ability classes, THCI scores, final hiking risk scores, route suitability scores, go/no-go decisions, medical diagnoses, or causality claims.
