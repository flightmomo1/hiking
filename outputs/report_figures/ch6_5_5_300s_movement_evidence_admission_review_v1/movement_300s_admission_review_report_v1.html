CH6.5.5 300s Movement Evidence Admission Review v1

PASS_CH6_5_5_300S_MOVEMENT_EVIDENCE_ADMISSION_REVIEW_V1_DESCRIPTIVE_ONLY

CH6.5.5 300s movement evidence admission review v1 is admission-review evidence only. It reviews corrected 300-second horizontal, vertical, HR, route-continuity, and artifact-guard evidence. It does not compute or authorize radar scores, ability scores, ability ranks, ability classes, THCI scores, final hiking risk scores, route suitability scores, go/no-go decisions, medical diagnoses, or causality claims.
