IB3W Station Representativeness Policy v1

Policy review only. No weather fusion, no risk score, no missing-to-zero imputation.

1. Scope and guardrails

Distance rank is not weather representativeness. Station elevation evidence is separate from station selection. Observation availability alone does not make a station representative of mountain-route weather.
